**Assignment Title** Studio 2.1
**Prompt** Medium A 
Assignment title: Visual Prompt Studio 2.1: The Material Transformation Challenge
Prompt: Create a watercolor painting of a solitary tree standing on a small hill beneath a dramatic evening sky. Emphasize line, shape, contrast, texture, movement, and emphasis. Use flowing organic lines for the branches, simplified shapes for the tree and landscape, strong contrast between the dark tree and luminous sky, and visible watercolor texture throughout the composition. Create movement through sweeping cloud formations and directional brushstrokes, with the tree as the main focal point.

**Prompt B** Medium B
Prompt B: Create a linocut print of the same tree standing on a small hill beneath a evening sky. Keep the same composition, subject placement, line, shape, contrast, texture, movement, and emphasis as the watercolor version. Use organic lines for the branches, simple shapes for the tree and landscape, strong contrast between positive and negative space, and carved textures for movement in the clouds and landscape. The tree should be the primary focal point.
Using a linoleum block, carving gouges, relief-printing ink, a brayer, and printmaking paper. Use the incising and carving process to remove areas of the linoleum and create positive and negative shapes. Carve directional lines into the clouds, branches, and landscape to create movement and texture. Apply ink evenly with a brayer and transfer the image onto paper as a high-contrast relief print. It should be the same overall composition as the watercolor, but allow the limitations of carved lines, flat ink areas, and positive/negative space to define the final appearance. The result should feel graphic, bold, tactile, and structured.
